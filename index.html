    Everything Cycler does.

    The ride above shows the main ideas. This is the full list, as of version 0.1.12.

    Ride

    Record with the screen off
    Live map, start, pause and finish. Distance, speed, moving time, average, max and climb, with auto-pause when you stop.
    Follow a route
    Ride any saved or planned route with distance to go, the next turn, a nudge before each turn and a vibration if you leave the line.
    Laps and interval workouts
    A Lap button and optional auto-lap. Interval workouts with on-screen, vibration and spoken prompts for each block.
    Big-number ride screen
    Swipeable pages of large numbers, with the fields you choose.
    Weather and daylight
    Wind, temperature and rain chance on the map, and a sunset warning before you start.
    Offline maps
    Save the map around you for rides without signal. Recording itself never needs the internet.

    Plan

    Plan A to B
    Start and end fields, optional stops, search or long-press the map. Up to three routes with distance, climb, time, difficulty and an elevation profile.
    Plan a loop
    Pick a distance from 5 to 200 km and get round trips in four directions, or ask for short, medium and long loops near you.

    Look back

    Rides feed
    Every finished ride with stats, route shape, photos, how it felt, notes and tags. Filter by week, month or year, sort, search and filter by tag.
    Ride pages
    Photos and videos, laps, stops, stopped time, signal count, heart rate, and optional advanced speed, elevation and effort detail.
    Activity and records
    One chart by week, month or year for distance, time or climb, with totals, records and best efforts, and all rides on one map.
    Segments
    Cut a stretch from any ride. Every ride over it is timed and ranked.
    Share cards and flyover
    A card in 13 swipeable styles with editable title, numbers, photo and size, or an animated 3D flyover video of the ride.
    Bike care
    Jobs with kilometre or day intervals, notes and history. Mark one done today, on an earlier date or after a past ride.
    Home-screen widgets
    Ride, This week, Last ride and Riding stats. Start, pause and lap a ride without opening the app.

    Your data

    GPX import, FIT, TCX and GPX export
    Bring routes in as GPX. Send rides out as FIT, TCX or GPX files to any app that imports them.
    Health Connect
    Reads heart rate and weight that other apps put in Health Connect. Read only.
    Backup and restore
    Rides, routes, photos, segments, bike care and settings in one zip file that you keep.

    What leaves your phone. All five things.

    Rides, photos and settings stay in the app's own storage. Maps, weather and routing have to come from somewhere, so here is every request Cycler makes and what it carries.

    • No account
    • No analytics
    • No ads
    • No tracking
    • No cloud backup
    Map tilesOpenFreeMap, CyclOSMThe tiles you view, so the provider sees the area.
    WeatherOpen-MeteoYour position rounded to about 100 m, at most every 15 minutes.
    Route planningBRouterThe waypoints of the route or loop you ask for.
    Place searchPhotonThe text you type, and your position rounded to about 1 km.
    Traffic signalsOverpass APIA bounding box around the route you open or follow.

    This website has no analytics either. Its planner uses the same servers. Map tiles from OpenFreeMap and the weather line from Open-Meteo load when you reach the planner. Your search text goes to Photon and the loop's waypoints go to BRouter only when you plan a loop of your own.

    These are free community servers with no API keys. A backup is a zip file you save yourself. Full policy: docs/PRIVACY.md.

    Questions people ask before installing.

    Short answers. The longer ones are in the Cycler README.

    Is Cycler free?

    Yes. Cycler is free and open source under the MIT licence, with no ads.

    Do I need an account to use Cycler?

    No. There is no sign-up and no login. Rides, photos and settings stay in the app's own storage on your phone.

    Does Cycler work without internet?

    Recording does not need the internet. Maps, route planning and weather do, so save the map around you for offline use before a ride without signal.

    Does it keep recording with the screen off?

    Yes. Cycler records in the background with the screen off. If Android stops the app during a ride, recording restarts by itself and keeps your laps.

    Can I move my rides to Strava, Garmin Connect or another app?

    Export any ride as a FIT, TCX or GPX file and import that file into any app or site that accepts those formats.

    Does Cycler read heart rate from my watch?

    Cycler reads heart rate and weight that other apps put in Health Connect. It is read only and nothing is written back.

    Is Cycler on Google Play?

    Not yet, but soon. Cycler is on its way to Google Play. Until then it is available as an APK on its GitHub Releases page.

    How do I update Cycler?

    Cycler does not check for updates. Download a newer release from the Releases page and install it over the old one. Your rides are kept.

    Is there an iPhone version?

    Not yet. Publishing on iPhone needs a paid Apple developer licence, and Cycler is free with no income behind it, so Android comes first. If enough donations come in to cover that cost, an iPhone version will follow soon after. You can help at buymeacoffee.com/abhiramnj. Today Cycler needs Android 8.0 or later.

    How do I back up my rides or move to a new phone?

    In Settings, open Maps and data and choose Back up everything. You get one zip file with your rides, routes, photos, segments, bike care and settings, which you can restore on another phone.

    How is Cycler paid for, and can I support it?

    Cycler is free, with no ads and nothing to unlock, and it is built in spare time. If it is useful to you, you can pay it forward with a coffee at buymeacoffee.com/abhiramnj. That money goes to what the app costs to build and publish, and keeps it free for the next rider. Support is optional and changes nothing in the app.

    What data leaves my phone?

    Only five kinds of request: map tiles, weather, route planning, place search and traffic signals. There is no account, no analytics and no tracking. The privacy section on this page lists what each request sends.
