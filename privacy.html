Privacy policy

What happens to your data.

Cycler is built so your rides stay yours. This page says exactly what stays on your phone, what leaves it, and why.

Effective 26 November 2026. Applies to the Cycler app and to this website.

In short

  • Cycler has no account, no ads and no cloud. Your rides, photos and settings stay in the app's own storage on your phone.
  • The developer cannot see your rides. Nothing is uploaded to a server we run.
  • The app talks to a handful of free community services for maps, weather and routing. They are listed below, with what each one receives.
  • Beyond those, Cycler keeps the lightest possible anonymous usage counts. They carry no name, no rides and no locations, and they are never used for advertising or sold.
  • A backup is a file you save yourself. Delete a ride or uninstall the app and it is gone.

Who is responsible

Cycler is made by Abhiram N J and published by Kunj Company ("we", "us"). We decide what Cycler does with data and are the contact for anything in this policy. You can reach us at hellokunjcompany@gmail.com.

This policy covers the Cycler app for Android and this website. It is written to be read, not skimmed past: if something here does not match what you see the app doing, tell us and we will fix one or the other.

What stays on your phone

Rides, GPS points, routes, photos and videos you attach, notes, tags, segments, bike care records and settings are stored in the app's own storage on your device. The developer cannot see them. Android's cloud backup is turned off for Cycler, so they are not copied to your Google account either.

What leaves your phone

Maps, weather and routing have to come from somewhere. Cycler makes five kinds of request, all over HTTPS, to free community services. None of them needs an account or an API key.

Like any web server, these services receive your IP address, and they have their own privacy policies. Cycler sends them nothing else and does not add any identifier of its own.

Anonymous usage counts

Cycler keeps the lightest possible anonymous usage counts, just enough to know the app is being used and where it breaks. We use them to decide what to fix and what to build next.

  • What they are: simple counts, such as how often the app is opened, which app version and Android version are in use, and whether a feature or an error happened.
  • What they never contain: your name or email, your rides, routes, photos or notes, your location, your contacts, or an advertising identifier.
  • What we do not do: we do not build a profile of you, follow you across other apps or sites, show ads, or sell or share this data for marketing.

Because they are anonymous, we cannot look up or delete the counts of one particular person. If you would rather not take part, tell us at hellokunjcompany@gmail.com and we will say how to opt out of the current version.

Location

Cycler uses your precise location to show you on the map and to record rides. Recording can continue with the screen off and shows a notification while it runs. Your location is not sent anywhere except as described in the table above, and in rounded form where the table says so.

Health Connect

If you choose to connect Health Connect, Cycler reads heart rate and weight, only to show heart rate on ride pages and to estimate energy. Access is read only: Cycler never writes to Health Connect. What it reads stays on your phone and is not shared with anyone.

To revoke access, open Android Settings, then Health Connect, then App permissions, then Cycler, and turn off Heart rate and Weight, or remove the app's access. You can also revoke it from Cycler's Settings.

Backups, photos and sharing

Back up everything creates a zip file that you save wherever you choose. It holds rides, routes, photos, segments, bike care and settings. Photos keep their original metadata (EXIF), including where they were taken, because it is not removed. Keep the file as private as the photos themselves. Restore reads such a file back in.

Exports and share cards. Exports (FIT, TCX, GPX), share cards and videos are created on your phone and go only where you send them with Android's share sheet. Once you send a file to another app or person, their policy applies to their copy.

This website

The website is a static site. We do not run accounts, forms or a database on it, and its own code does not set cookies or store anything in your browser to identify you. It may count visits anonymously, in the same lightest-touch way as the app, and nothing more.

To work, the page loads a few things from other providers. Each sees your IP address and your browser's details when it serves you a file:

  • Hosting: the site is served by Vercel, which keeps ordinary server logs for security and operation.
  • Fonts: Google Fonts serves the typefaces.
  • Animation code: the cdnjs content network (Cloudflare) serves the GSAP animation library.
  • The planner on the home page: map tiles from OpenFreeMap and the weather line from Open-Meteo load when you reach it. Your search text goes to Photon and the loop's waypoints go to BRouter only when you plan a loop of your own.
  • Links out: GitHub, Buy Me a Coffee and our other sites are separate services with their own policies. We do not receive anything from them about you, other than what a payment provider tells us about a donation (see the Terms).

The share card on the home page is drawn in your browser. It is not uploaded anywhere.

How long data is kept

Data stays on your phone until you delete a ride or uninstall the app. Uninstalling removes everything Cycler stored, except files you saved yourself, such as a backup. We hold no copy of your rides and so have nothing to delete on your behalf. Anonymous counts are kept only as long as they are useful.

Your rights

Your rides and photos are yours and already in your hands: you can view, export, move or delete them in the app at any time. For anything else, depending on where you live you may have the right to ask what data we hold about you, to have it corrected or deleted, to object to how it is used, and to complain to your data protection authority. Because we hold no personal data about you beyond anonymous counts and any message you send us, there is usually little to look up, but ask at hellokunjcompany@gmail.com and we will answer in good faith.

The services in the table above act on their own account. To exercise rights over what they hold, contact them directly.

Children

Cycler is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, write to us and we will delete it.
