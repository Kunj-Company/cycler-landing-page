Ride safely

Cycling carries a real risk of injury. Cycler is a tool to record and plan rides, not a safety system. You alone are responsible for how, where and when you ride.

  • Obey traffic laws and local rules, and ride within your ability and the conditions.
  • Keep your eyes on the road. Set up your route before you start and avoid handling your phone while moving. Recording works with the screen off for this reason.
  • Wear a helmet and use lights where they are needed.
  • Do not rely on Cycler in an emergency. Carry what you need to call for help, and do not depend on the app to notify anyone.

Maps, routes and weather

Check the way for yourself, and choose a different path whenever the road or your judgement says so.
